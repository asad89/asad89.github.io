Case study 01 Mobile / AI-assisted creation

Video editing,
without the editing degree.

NomadCut is an AI-assisted video editor for creators who want a polished short-form video without learning a professional timeline. The experience turns intent into a first edit, then keeps people in control through conversational changes and manual tools.

RoleProduct / UX / UI design
ScopeEnd-to-end mobile experience
AudienceEveryday creators & storytellers
FocusAI trust, control & recovery

The challenge

Editing tools expose complexity before they create confidence.

New creators often know the story they want to tell, but not the language of cuts, pacing, aspect ratios or timelines. The design challenge was to make AI feel helpful without making its decisions feel mysterious—or final.

The design response

Start with intent. Reveal complexity only when it becomes useful.

A guided brief translates creative direction into understandable choices. NomadCut then produces a reviewable first edit, explains credit costs before an AI action, and always offers a manual path for precise control.

Core journey

One clear path from raw clips to a shareable story.

  1. 01Choose

    Upload existing media or capture with AI coaching.

  2. 02Brief

    Describe the story, platform, duration and creative tone.

  3. 03Review

    Preview a complete first edit before committing to export.

  4. 04Refine

    Ask AI for changes or move into hands-on editing.

  5. 05Export

    Finish with format and quality choices made explicit.

UX decisions

Designing for trust—not just speed.

01

Make AI costs visible

Credit usage appears before generation and revision actions, reducing surprise and supporting informed decisions.

02

Keep a human in the loop

The first edit is a checkpoint—not an endpoint. People can accept, regenerate, talk to AI or edit manually.

03

Use progressive disclosure

Simple creative choices lead the experience; timeline tools appear only when someone asks for granular control.

04

Design recovery states

Missing clips, low credits and failed dependencies are treated as recoverable tasks with clear next actions.

Prototype outcome

A complete product story—not a collection of polished screens.

The concept demonstrates the full lifecycle of an AI creative task: entry, instruction, system feedback, review, refinement, exception handling and export. The next step would be usability testing the brief, credit comprehension and the handoff between AI revision and manual editing.
